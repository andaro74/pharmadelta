## Which scenario each label serves

- lisinopril: scenario 1 (pregnancy, 201.57(c)(9)(i)); its boxed
  warning is about pregnancy.
- metformin: scenario 3 (RLD label changed, ANDA must follow) and
  scenario 4 (buried adverse event). See the finding below.
- metoprolol tartrate: scenario 2 (superseded rule) and the ordinary
  question.

## Finding: a live RLD divergence on metformin

On 2026-10-09 the extended-release metformin label on DailyMed carried
"RECENT MAJOR CHANGES: Boxed Warning 08/2026" and listed "mitochondrial
diseases" among the lactic-acidosis risk factors. The Quallent
plain-tablet label (page updated 2025-12-04; label text revision date
10/2022) does not list it. If the
plain-tablet reference product (NDA 020357) carries the same 08/2026
change, this is a real scenario 3: the RLD's label changed and this
ANDA's label has not followed. To confirm at M00: open the NDA 020357
label on DailyMed and compare its boxed warning. Not yet confirmed.
