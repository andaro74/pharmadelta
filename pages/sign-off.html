pharmadelta is an independent open-source demo and is not affiliated with any pharmaceutical company. The company, Ardentia Generics, is fictional; its portfolio and every “our label” statement are invented. The labels are real public-domain labels of old off-patent generics, read from DailyMed.

This page holds the reviewer sign-off and the evidence package: who reviewed the evidence behind this demo's ledger (milestones/README.md), under which seat, and what they signed; and the package a reader downloads to read the same evidence. Whether that commit is the seat holder's, and whether the package is what CI wrote with every entry equal to its source, is read back by the M06 gate on every CI run (m06.yml runs); this page claims neither.

Built in this repository's website layer. The sign-off is not an agentkeel seat and not a platform control, and the platform does not read it (SPEC/00 §5). The agent is label-impact, a tenant of agentkeel; its seven seats are read from its manifest.yaml. One person holds all seven seats on this project (agentkeel ruling R1, ADR-0001), so the reviewer is the author of every pull request in this repository and reviews their own work; the sign-off's worth is that this repository's CI can read it back, not that the reviewer is independent (M06 Unsure 10). Pharmacovigilance and Legal hold no seat and never appear here as a signature (SPEC/00 §5).
