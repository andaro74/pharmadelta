pharmadelta is an independent open-source demo and is not affiliated with any pharmaceutical company. The company, Ardentia Generics, is fictional; its portfolio and every “our label” statement are invented. The label is a real public-domain label of an old off-patent generic, read from DailyMed.

Every answer on this page was produced by a call to the deployed agent, label-impact, through its door as the caller pharmadelta, made by this repository's CI: https://github.com/andaro74/pharmadelta/actions/runs/43604067436, artifact m03-lift-4bfa4d884a4b43299e59169fe0c1532b9dd922a8. The question

FDA has published a revision to 21 CFR 201.57(c)(9)(i), the content rule for section 8.1 Pregnancy. Does it touch our lisinopril tablet label, and what do we owe?

The answer

Row lisinopril.8.1 under clause 201.57(c)(9)(i), as the agent answered on the call recorded below.

The authority for what this demo claims is SPEC/00-overview.md; what was measured is in milestones/README.md. No number on this page is a measured result: the row's and the clause's numbers are their own data, and the call record's are the CI run's, linked above.
